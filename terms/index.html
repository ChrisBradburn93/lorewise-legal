4. What Lorewise provides

Lorewise teaches history, etymology and folklore through a trading-card mechanic. Its subject matter includes British and Irish history, world history, American history, folklore and cryptids.

The app has two separate layers:

  • Reading. You may read knowledge cards, save favourites and take short quizzes. Reading a card does not mean that you own it.
  • Collecting. You receive sealed digital "packs" on a daily cadence and open them. The cards inside then enter your permanent collection, called your Binder. Only cards obtained from packs count as owned. A cosmetic "foil" upgrade system also applies to owned cards.

Lorewise also includes a second study mode, Scholar's Assignments, consisting of structured courses and investigations that teach a subject and how to weigh historical sources. Completing one awards a card that cannot be obtained in any other way.

Lorewise has no leaderboards, no competitive leagues, no lives or hearts, no advertising and no social feed.

7.3 Pack contents and odds

Each pack contains 5 cards. Four are drawn from the subjects you follow: 2 Common, 1 Uncommon, and 1 card from the rarest slot, which resolves as set out below.

Where the subjects you follow cannot supply a card of the rarity a slot calls for, we draw one of that rarity from the wider library instead, so that the odds below hold whatever you happen to follow. Only if the library cannot supply one either do we fall back to a Common. A card you receive by either route is yours on exactly the same terms as any other: you may read it, it enters your collection, and it counts toward badges and achievements, even if it comes from a Group you have not unlocked.

The fifth card depends on your tier:

  • Founder: a discovery card, drawn from a subject you do not follow, which you may choose whether to add to your collection. A discovery card is currently always a Common or an Uncommon. That range is a feature of the app rather than a term of this contract, and we may change it.
  • Free: an additional Common from the Groups you can access. Discovery cards are drawn from subjects outside the Groups you follow, and are therefore a Founder feature.

The rarest slot is the rarest card in the pack, and it carries the following odds. Most of the time it is a Rare:

  • Legendary: 0.6%
  • Epic: 14%
  • Rare: 85.4%

A pity threshold also applies: after 150 consecutive packs in which you have received no Legendary, the next card in the rarest slot is guaranteed to be Legendary.

Duplicates. Cards are drawn independently each time, and we do not exclude cards you already own. You will therefore receive duplicates, and the more of a Group you have collected the more often that will happen. Duplicates are not waste: they are the only source of foil upgrades and Lore Fragments. We do not publish a duplicate rate because it depends on how much of your accessible pool you already hold, and any single figure we quoted would be wrong for most players.

These odds are shown in the app, on the Founder purchase screen, before you buy, so that you can see them before deciding.
