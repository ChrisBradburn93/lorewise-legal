Lorewise

Lorewise is an app for learning history, etymology and folklore through collectible cards. Each card is a short, carefully sourced piece about one person, event, object, place, word or legend, written for readers who want to know why it matters.

What you can do in Lorewise

  • Read. Swipe through cards on the subjects you choose, from British history to ancient Rome, Norse myth and the origins of everyday words.
  • Collect. Open packs of cards and build a collection in your own Binder.
  • Study. Follow Scholar's Assignments, short courses and investigations that teach a subject from start to finish and show how to judge a historical source.

Who it is for

Lorewise is an app for adults and for readers aged 16 and over. It has no advertising. Some subjects are grim, so cards about violence, sexual content and atrocity are hidden unless an adult reader chooses to see them.

Availability

Lorewise is for Android and is currently in testing on Google Play.

Signing in

You can create an account with an email address or sign in with your Google account. Lorewise uses Google sign-in only to create your account and sign you in. It does not ask Google for anything beyond your basic profile and email address. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and how to delete it.
